## Smallest eligible shape

Run an authority-free gate first: one frozen semantic request plus its bound camera frame, two models by two perception conditions, three calls per cell, concurrency one, and no retry, repair, fallback, or changed action contract. A model enters the live comparison only if every condition produces at least one public schema-valid action or yield inside the fixed latency bound. A malformed result is data, not something to tune away.

Only if all four cells pass, create four isolated sibling histories from one stopped checkpoint and run them sequentially through the ordinary Place/Behold path. Permit at most two natural resident decisions per branch: an initial choice and, if Minecraft returns a consequence before the bound, one later choice that may or may not take it up. Stop on the resident's first yield, route/parser/camera failure, the decision cap, or the sealed wall bound. Do not stop for repetition, silence, bad judgment, or lack of progress. The second choice is branch-local consequence uptake, not a matched-world causal estimate after trajectories diverge.

Seal one content-addressed manifest before the first call: the question and decision it could reverse; code and world/checkpoint identities; four cells; exact semantic projection, frame renderer/FOV/encoding/freshness, models/templates/runtime/settings, prefix/action/schema/parser hashes, isolated sessions, order, budgets, and raw descriptive measures.
