# Separate model effects from perception effects in one sealed comparison

Candidate diagnostic, not an ordinary-product blocker. If ordinary use leaves a decision that this comparison can resolve, use explicit cloned world lineage to vary model and semantic-only versus exact-camera perception independently. Supply no task, preferred action, recovery script, or success grade. The comparison diagnoses the interface and does not become the resident product. Do not run it merely because the matrix can be filled.

## Acceptance Criteria

Routes models world snapshots resident prefixes budgets parsers sampling conditions stop rules and observational measures are fixed before paid or live calls; both models receive matched conditions with isolated sessions; results report latency invalid attempts action distribution repeated trajectories consequence uptake social events and later recognition without winner or conduct grading; negative results remain preserved; no canonical resident life is silently forked or overwritten.

## Smallest eligible shape

Run an authority-free gate first: one frozen semantic request plus its bound camera frame, two models by two perception conditions, three calls per cell, concurrency one, and no retry, repair, fallback, or changed action contract. A model enters the live comparison only if every condition produces at least one public schema-valid action or yield inside the fixed latency bound. A malformed result is data, not something to tune away.

Only if all four cells pass, create four isolated sibling histories from one stopped checkpoint and run them sequentially through the ordinary Place/Behold path. Permit at most two natural resident decisions per branch: an initial choice and, if Minecraft returns a consequence before the bound, one later choice that may or may not take it up. Stop on the resident's first yield, route/parser/camera failure, the decision cap, or the sealed wall bound. Do not stop for repetition, silence, bad judgment, or lack of progress. The second choice is branch-local consequence uptake, not a matched-world causal estimate after trajectories diverge.

Seal one content-addressed manifest before the first call: the question and decision it could reverse; code and world/checkpoint identities; four cells; exact semantic projection, frame renderer/FOV/encoding/freshness, models/templates/runtime/settings, prefix/action/schema/parser hashes, isolated sessions, order, budgets, and raw descriptive measures. Reuse existing request, release, checkpoint, journal, and Lync artifacts. Do not create a new evaluation framework or a winner score.
