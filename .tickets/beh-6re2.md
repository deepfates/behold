# Let legible residents keep a self-chosen concern across actions

Episode 000015 converted social looping into locally adaptive locomotion but no durable consequence. Ground truth shows self.projects is present in the human-semantic observation and ProjectMemory is instantiated, yet minecraft-human-semantic-v1 filters out manage_project. Legible residents can publish only one-step commitments, so their existing durable concern module is readable but unwritable.

## Design

Compose the existing project-memory utility beside the frozen human-semantic bodily action surface for product legible residents; do not widen the neutral benchmark or add a supplied task/goal. Keep one bounded self-chosen concern and reuse the existing no-bookkeeping-without-world-progress guard.

## Acceptance Criteria

A legible untasked resident may autonomously create and later read its own bounded ongoing concern without changing the frozen bodily affordance definitions or neutral benchmark. In a bounded ordinary live exercise, a resident chooses such a concern and takes at least two causally coherent later actions toward it, including a material world-action attempt, with the concern surviving its Lync history; no task or next action is supplied.
