# Exercise current resident and native-human coexistence together

Compose the supported current resident contract, multiple independently minded residents, shared physical weights where applicable, and a native Java human in the same ordinary persistent world. Close the current compositional evidence gap without prescribing what anyone does.

## Design

Joining, watching, speaking, leaving, and returning are habitat events, not a staged exchange or resident-release ritual. Preserve per-resident controllers, contexts, quotas, bodies, and Lync lives even when weights are shared.

## Acceptance Criteria

One ordinary current-profile episode and its clean resume include at least two residents and an authenticated native-player join witnessed by the server and every resident. The human can play normally; residents remain unsteered and may act, yield, ignore, fail, die, or interact. Requests and histories demonstrate no cross-resident context leakage, Minecraft changes persist, shared weights do not merge sessions, and Textile reads each resulting life.
