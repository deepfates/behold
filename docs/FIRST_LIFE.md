# First Life

First Life is Behold's smallest meaningful current capability: start a resident
in a persistent Minecraft place, watch its embodied causal loop, stop the
runtime, and return to the same world and private life. It is a bounded vertical
slice of the [product horizon](PRODUCT_HORIZON.md), not the endpoint itself.

## Intended endpoint

Behold's owner-ratified horizon is a persistent, ecologically active world that
a person can enter and that multiple independently minded residents can
inhabit, change, and leave readable histories from. Behold is responsible for
the truth and operability of that habitat. It is not responsible for making a
resident wise, productive, social, survivable, or interesting.

## Why a life is harder than a task

A task can be passed by a disposable controller. A life has unfinished
concerns: it must remain the same life through consequences, interruption, and
return. First Life therefore preserves the conditions for continuity rather
than grading whether a short task was completed.

Caring for a body, beginning a project, responding to another inhabitant, or
leaving a recognizable change are phenomena a capable resident might exhibit.
A resident may instead repeat itself, remain inactive, fail, die, or pursue
something opaque without making the habitat false. Behold is responsible for
whether that conduct had truthful information, available controls, real
consequences, private continuity, and enough time to unfold.

## Implemented capability

The ordinary `behold live` path currently:

- verifies a clean Behold checkout, an installed or pinned Place Compiler, a
  schema-v3 Place release with qualified living entry, a pinned Minecraft
  server JAR, resident configurations, and cognition routes before starting;
- materializes and serves the release as one session-owned persistent world;
- starts one or more separately configured resident bodies with bounded
  provider-attempt quotas and isolated private Lync lives;
- supplies resident-visible semantic experience and only currently admitted
  player-shaped actions, then records Minecraft's returned consequence;
- exposes loopback POV viewers, a read-only resident lens, and the native
  Minecraft server endpoint;
- drains cognition, saves Minecraft, closes owned processes, and records an
  authenticated episode on a normal duration boundary, lens stop, or Ctrl-C;
- resumes the same saved world head, bodies, resident configuration, and
  selected Lync lives from the printed command; and
- provides an evidence-preserving recovery command for a precisely classified
  abandoned stopped epoch. Recovery does not start Minecraft or a model.

Resident policy may produce zero or one bodily intention for a cognition
opportunity. A null intention creates no fabricated Minecraft action or
consequence. Action and explicit non-action cognition events share the
resident's canonical private chronology.

## Exercised evidence

The current clean-checkout candidate has run from a disposable fresh clone on
the workshop machine. It used a separately installed Place Compiler, a private
qualified deterministic-entry Oxford starter, the tracked OpenRouter resident,
and a newly created FirstResident life. An automated unmanaged Minecraft
visitor asked one question; the resident produced one terrain-grounded reply
recorded by Minecraft. The episode saved and stopped cleanly, and a later
ordinary invocation reopened the same world, body, route, and private Lync
life.

The retained [2026-08-22 First Life report](reports/2026-08-22-first-life-local-installed-resume.md)
contains the exact identities, commands, failures, costs, digests, and
nonclaims. Earlier dated reports retain independently useful experiments in
population isolation, native-client coexistence, action truth, finite-context
continuity, recovery, and reader compatibility. They are evidence about their
recorded revisions and treatments, not current status pages.

Repository tests exercise the admission, lifecycle, authority, resident
contract, lens, Lync, and recovery mechanics. They support those bounded claims;
they do not establish that a new person can use the system or that a habitat is
worth inhabiting.

## Unknown, incomplete, or unsupported

- The starter release is private and has no durable public coordinate.
- No independent person or machine has followed the ordinary entry.
- The current branch has not established cross-platform operation.
- Repeated multi-day operation on the current composition remains unfinished.
  Earlier long episodes are pressure evidence, not acceptance of the current
  treatment.
- Resident competence, varied conduct, survival, relationships, culture, and
  sustained ecological value remain observations or research questions, not
  Behold guarantees.
- `behold live` does not launch a person's native Minecraft client or invoke
  Textile; it prints the endpoint and preserves readable Lync sources.
- Ordinary privacy settings preserve hashes, attribution, causal turns, and
  usage but not literal provider request/response bodies. Exact model-I/O
  inspection is opt-in and sensitive.
